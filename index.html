The basics.

Does Stadiate stream the actual game?+

No. Keep your usual TV service. Stadiate does not include broadcast rights or overlay other Apple TV apps. It shows scores on a companion screen. The demo’s prerecorded play is supplied with permission. Optional Mac capture needs your own unprotected source, ffmpeg, and macOS permissions; DRM still applies.

Will it spoil a delayed broadcast?+

Use the built-in delay and SYNC on your phone to match your stream. Team and fantasy-player celebrations wait for the same game delay. On standalone Apple TV, pair your phone by QR code on the same Wi-Fi.

What connects to the Mac app?+

Browser boards and Apple TV boards, plus supported Hue, Govee LAN, Home Assistant, and Sonos devices. The Mac app runs locally while your Mac is awake. Sports data needs internet; integrations may need their own accounts. No Stadiate cloud account is required. Privacy policy.
